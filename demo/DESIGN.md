## Brand & Style

This design system blends crisp technical reliability with an elevated, climate-native tropical energy aesthetic. Designed for high-end residential owners, regional enterprise hospitality, and island utility microgrids, it communicates hurricane-grade resilience and effortless abundance.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Modern Minimalism** with **Tactile/Tonal Depth**:
- **Radiant & Precise:** Generous open breathing room mimics bright ocean daylight, punctuated by high-contrast typographic hierarchy and sunlit metallic accents.
- **Resilient Trust:** Infrastructure-grade clarity with polished details avoids raw industrial austerity, leaning instead into architectural elegance reminiscent of sustainable Caribbean coastal architecture.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Subtle radiant gradients and luminescent state accents evoke stored solar power, coastal trade winds, and high-efficiency battery telemetry.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the environment: high-efficiency photovoltaic absorption, sun-drenched coastal vistas, and deep maritime stability.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Teal (`#007A87`):** Represents maritime depth and infrastructural precision. Applied to high-priority interactive triggers, core navigation, and brand markers.
- **Secondary Solar Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Captures kinetic solar generation and stored reserve capacity. Used strategically for metrics, energy flow badges, and active operational indicators.
- **Tertiary Sky Cyan (`#0EA5E9`):** Represents grid connectivity, live monitoring streams, and active state highlights.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** Anchors the design with crisp contrast across headlines, borders, and structural frames.

### Supporting Tone System
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Luminous crystalline white with cool undertone).
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp clean white).
- **Surface Elevation 2:** `#F1F5F9` (Muted ocean mist for table heads and recessed containers).
- **Subdued Text:** `#475569` (Balanced readability without competing against core data metrics).
- **Functional Status:**
  - *Grid Active / Safe:* `#059669` (Verdant coastal green)
  - *Telemetry Warning / Depleted Reserve:* `#DC2626` (Direct signal red)

## Typography

The type scale balances the clean-tech structural engineering of **Space Grotesk** with the ergonomic warmth and readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Provides technological poise, forward-looking geometric clarity, and distinctive architectural personality. Tight negative tracking keeps larger sizes muscular and modern.
- **Body & Longform (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Brings soft humanism, high legibility across bright display environments, and comfortable scanning for complex energy audits, telemetry logs, and bill breakdowns.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Space Grotesk is leveraged with slight uppercase tracking for sensor data, hardware specs, and kilowatt metrics, reinforcing instrument-grade precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, utilizing structured fluid grids with expansive margins to project confidence, prestige, and institutional scale.

### Grid Parameters
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, 2rem (32px) gutters, generous 3rem to 4.5rem exterior frame padding. Max content container capped at 1360px to preserve visual scanability.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, 1.5rem (24px) gutters, 2rem margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, 1rem (16px) gutters, 1rem screen margins.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Maintain large vertical step separations (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) between key product sections to prevent visual crowding.
- Keep dashboard metrics and electrical parameter groups tightly coupled (`space-xs` to `space-md`) inside self-contained modules to preserve cognitive cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects muddy, heavy dropshadows in favor of crisp daylight luminescence and ocean-tinted ambient diffusions.

### Tiers of Depth
- **Level 0 (Floor/Canvas):** Base background `#F8FAFC`, flat, anchoring all content.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 122, 135, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards / Hover):** Elevated cards lifted above the canvas: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 122, 135, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Border transitions to `rgba(0, 122, 135, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Controls):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16), 0 0 0 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

### Solar Luminescence (Glow Accents)
When energy generation is active or primary system actions are highlighted, elements receive a directional warm solar aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design uses roundedness factor **2 (Rounded)**:
- Standard UI modules, inputs, and interactive surfaces use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Larger layout cards and feature modules use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Modals, hero media wraps, and telemetry stat groups scale to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Pills (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for contextual status indicators, hardware telemetry badges (e.g., "99.8% Online"), and micro-toggles.

This balanced curvature pairs naturally with the geometric lines of solar arrays and battery cabinets without feeling playful or ungrounded.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#007A87` fill, pure white Space Grotesk 14px bold typography, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Hover transforms to deep cyan-teal with subtle lift and `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(0, 122, 135, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Solar Action:** Solid `#F59E0B` fill, `#0F172A` text. Used exclusively for energy engagement, consultation booking, and solar yield calculators.
- **Tertiary Outline:** Transparent canvas, `1.5px solid #007A87`, `#007A87` text. Hover fills with 6% opacity teal tint.

### Cards
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, framed with `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- Padding scales from `1.5rem` (mobile) to `2rem` (desktop).
- Header groups display bold Space Grotesk section titles with uppercase micro-labels above in `#0EA5E9`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Height: 24px–28px, full pill shape (`9999px`).
- In-Service / Generating: Light green-tint surface (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)`), deep emerald label, with a pulsing 6px `#059669` dot.
- Solar Surplus: Light amber-tint surface (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`), deep amber label (`#B45309`).
- Island Grid Mode: Deep marine slate surface (`#0F172A`), white text, crisp technical icon.

### Form Inputs
- Surface: Crisp `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Height: 44px for balanced touch-target confidence. Inner padding: `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#0EA5E9` with a subtle outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.2)`. Label hangs above in Space Grotesk semi-bold (`13px`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18px x 18px.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: `#007A87` fill, white check icon, paired with a faint cyan glow ring.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Power Flow Monitor:** Dynamic SVG schematic showing bidirectional vector animation (Solar Array -> Hybrid Inverter -> Home / Island Grid -> LFP Battery Banks). Uses `#F59E0B` for production, `#0EA5E9` for distribution, and `#007A87` for reserve storage.
- **Battery Reserve Gauge:** Segmented vertical battery meter with linear gradient fill from `#007A87` (base charge) through `#0EA5E9` to `#F59E0B` (peak saturation), accompanied by a large metric numeral displaying reserve runtime (e.g., `38.5 hrs Off-Grid`).

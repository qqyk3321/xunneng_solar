All formal customer pages retain Analytics and Speed Insights. Submission URLs are redacted before telemetry; `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` and `noindex` remain. Sitemap contains the ten public pages only. The test-page index, experiments, child APIs and geocoding stubs are physically removed, without compatibility redirects or replacement middleware. Next.js supplies its normal 404 behavior.

`app/faq/faq-directory.tsx` and its styles are shared by regional FAQ pages; their presence does not create a `/faq` route. Quote algorithms, calculator code, contact storage/validation, editors, compression and rate limiting remain. Two unchanged image helper functions and their MIME type moved from `lib/image-lab/config.ts` to `lib/anonymous-inquiry/image.ts`; unused lab constants were removed. Only `@supabase/ssr` and its exclusive `cookie` dependency were pruned from the lockfile; no remaining package versions changed.

- Contact form: privacy gating and reset, rich editor, actual image compression/upload payload, regional country code, failed-response recovery and stable retry ID passed at desktop and mobile sizes. Browser POSTs were intercepted.
- Other-island registration validation, error recovery and inline success passed on desktop/mobile.
- Regional calculator: 24 cases (two regions × four currencies × three bill levels) matched outputs generated by the pre-cleanup release.
- Customer results: Antigua, Dominica and missing-input fixtures passed at 1440/390/320px; all three visible figures matched the baseline; original record frame, token/region protection and missing-input behavior passed. Changing only the loopback pricing fixture and reloading changed quote/payback while preserving saved electricity.
- Actual local Next API handlers, backed by an in-memory loopback service, passed origin checks, contact persistence/idempotency, wishlist persistence and authenticated cron. No real database records were written.
- Candidate browser telemetry checks covered all ten public pages, both synthetic submission routes and excluded routes; request bodies and Referer headers contained no test access tokens. Telemetry POSTs were intercepted. Post-promotion live smoke checks reconfirmed privacy, regional FAQ and both submission routes.

True Tally → production database → Resend delivery was not exercised. Its relevant unit tests passed and deployed functions/templates were unchanged. Tests did not send emails, create accounts, modify customer records or change live prices.

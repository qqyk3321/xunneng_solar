2.
Question: Can solar and batteries keep my appliances running during a power cut?
Answer: A correctly configured backup system can supply selected appliances using available solar and battery energy. What can run, and for how long, depends on the inverter, usable battery charge, sunlight and the appliances running together. Solar panels alone do not establish that you have outage backup, and we cannot guarantee whole-house or overnight air-conditioning backup without assessing the system.

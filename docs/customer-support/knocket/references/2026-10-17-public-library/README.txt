READ THESE FIRST
07 - Understanding Solar+Storage, Clean Energy Group, 2024, 73 pages.
     Best overall match for customer-side solar and battery questions.
     Covers design considerations, backup, value, retrofits, battery types,
     sizing, safety, lifetime and environmental issues.
01-06 - NETCC consumer guides, 39 pages combined.
     Six guides: installing solar, using solar, installing batteries, using
     batteries, home energy monitoring and going off grid.
     Practical consumer journeys rather than a physics textbook.
11 - To Catch the Sun, Lonny Grafman and Joshua Pearce, 2021, 207 pages.
     Deeper photovoltaic and electrical fundamentals, loads, batteries,
     planning and practical examples. Mainly small off-grid systems at or
     below 1 kW; this scope differs from residential hybrid installations.
08 - PV and Energy Storage O&M Best Practices, NREL and partners, 2018,
     153 pages. Professional reference for monitoring, maintenance,
     records and managing faults; includes larger installations.
09 - Toward Solar Photovoltaic Storm Resilience, DOE/FEMP, 2023, 31 pages.
     Particularly relevant background for Caribbean exposure and resilience.
10 - I Am Your Battery Storage Guide, NSW/OEH, 2016, 61 pages.
     Detailed storage concepts and a buyer's toolkit, primarily for businesses.
     Old market data and policy examples must not become current answers.

HOW TO BUILD THE CUSTOMER KNOWLEDGE BASE FROM THESE SOURCES
Keep company/service information as a separate authoritative layer.
Use the guides to identify missing topics and answerable customer questions.
Review relevant source sections for dates, audience and geographic limits.
Use confirmed company model datasheets, user manuals, monitoring guides and
warranty documents for product-specific answers. No company model list has
been established by this library, and example brands are not offered products.
Only publish adapted material with suitable reuse rights and review the output.
Do not import overseas rebates, export rules, old prices or generic warranties
as Sol & Tide promises. Validate ambiguous diagrams and numerical examples.
Test retrieval and answers before promoting an updated upload.

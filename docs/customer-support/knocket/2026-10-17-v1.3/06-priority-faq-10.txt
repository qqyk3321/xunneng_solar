2.
Question: Can solar and batteries keep my appliances running during a power cut?
Answer: A correctly configured backup system can supply selected appliances using available solar and battery energy. Backup depends on suitable equipment, isolation from the grid and the circuits included in the design. Available charge, sunlight and appliance loads determine what can run and for how long. Solar panels alone do not guarantee outage backup.

Question: Can a solar and battery system power my air conditioner, refrigerator and other appliances?
Answer: A suitably sized system can support appliances such as air conditioning, refrigerators, washing machines, lights, TVs and WiFi. The inverter and battery must suit the appliances running together, including starting demand and the energy needed over time. We need to assess your intended loads before confirming whole-house or overnight backup.

7.
Question: Do solar panels work on cloudy days or at night?
Answer: Panels can generate from daylight under cloud cover, usually with reduced output. At night, they do not have useful sunlight for household generation. Energy stored in a battery can be used after sunset within its available charge and power limits; the grid can cover a shortfall when available and supported by the configuration.

8.
Question: How long will a home battery keep my appliances running?
Answer: Runtime depends on usable stored energy, the starting charge, reserve settings, losses, the appliances running and any solar contribution. More appliances or higher power use generally shorten runtime. A battery's kWh rating alone does not guarantee a number of backup hours, and inverter power limits also matter. Share your intended backup appliances and operating times for an assessment.
